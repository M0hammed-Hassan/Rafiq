# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

Fully remote arrangements are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and require VP-level sign-off.

All remote work days must be logged in the HR portal by end of day.

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours: 10 AM to 4 PM, company local time.

Equipment (laptop, monitor) can be requested for home setup through the IT portal, subject to a $500 annual budget per employee.
